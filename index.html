Read this first

The regulation, BRD, internal systems and answer key are synthetic and were written for this prototype. Extraction rules and prompts were tuned on these same 14 requirements, so scores are optimistic and show that the method works end to end, not its accuracy on real documents. LLM steps used a hosted open-weight model and vary between runs. A requirement can be flagged for a different reason than the planted one. Internal-source suggestions come from a small synthetic document set.
